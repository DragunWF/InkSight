# InkSight

InkSight is a mobile application that uses AI to generate insights based on physical or digital journal entries. By combining Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and the Gemini API, InkSight enhances your written reflections—whether handwritten or typed—providing meaningful feedback, tone analysis, and thought validation to support your journey of self-improvement.

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Scan Physical Journals**  
  Take a photo of a handwritten journal entry or select one from your image gallery. OCR will extract the text for further processing.

- 📝 **Digital Entry Support**  
  Manually type or paste your digital journal entries directly into the app to receive AI-generated insights.

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Insight Generation**  
  Journal text is polished using Gemini API for clarity while preserving your original thoughts. Insights are then generated based on the refined content.

- 💬 **Reflective Chatbot**  
  Engage in conversations about your journal entries with an AI chatbot designed to help you reflect and grow.

- 🏷️ **Mood & Keyword Tags** _(Suggested Feature)_  
  Automatically assigns emotional tone or topic tags like “gratitude,” “planning,” or “confusion” to help categorize your reflections.

- 📚 **Entry History**  
  View and revisit all your previously scanned or submitted journal entries in one place.

## 🌱 Benefits

- Acts as a personal feedback mechanism to improve your writing and expression over time.
- Helps validate and correct misunderstandings in thought, reinforcing clarity and growth.
- Encourages intentional reflection through AI-powered analysis and conversation.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

- **React Native** – Cross-platform mobile app development.
- **Expo** – Development and build tooling.
- **TypeScript** - Superset of JavaScript that adds static typing for better code quality.
- **SQLite** – Local storage for journal entries.
- **Gemini API** – Text polishing and insight generation.
- **OCR Library** – For recognizing text from physical journal images.
